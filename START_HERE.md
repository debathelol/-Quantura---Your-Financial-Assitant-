## 🎯 Three Simple Steps

### Step 1: Upload to GitHub (10 minutes)

1. **Create GitHub Account** (if you don't have one)
   - Go to [github.com](https://github.com)
   - Click "Sign up"
   - Choose a professional username

2. **Create New Repository**
   - Click the "+" icon → "New repository"
   - Name: `financial-analytics-dashboard`
   - Description: "AI-powered financial analytics dashboard with stock analysis, portfolio management, and personal finance tools"
   - Make it **PUBLIC** (required for free hosting!)
   - Click "Create repository"

3. **Upload Your Files**
   - Click "uploading an existing file"
   - Drag ALL files from this project
   - **IMPORTANT:** Rename `requirements-github.txt` to `requirements.txt`
   - Commit message: "Initial commit"
   - Click "Commit changes"

### Step 2: Deploy for FREE (5 minutes)

1. **Go to Streamlit Cloud**
   - Visit [share.streamlit.io](https://share.streamlit.io)
   - Click "Continue with GitHub"
   - Authorize Streamlit

2. **Deploy Your App**
   - Click "New app"
   - Repository: `yourusername/financial-analytics-dashboard`
   - Branch: `main`
   - Main file: `app.py`
   - Click "Advanced settings"

3. **Add API Key**
   - In "Secrets" section, paste:
     ```toml
     OPENAI_API_KEY = "your-key-here"
     ```
   - Get your free key from [platform.openai.com](https://platform.openai.com)
   - Click "Deploy"

4. **Wait 2-3 Minutes**
   - Your app is building...
   - You'll get a URL like: `https://yourusername-financial-analytics.streamlit.app`

### Step 3: Test & Share (5 minutes)

1. **Test Your Live App**
   - Try stock analysis (enter "Apple")
   - Upload sample CSV
   - Test AI chat

2. **Update README**
   - Add your deployed URL to the "Live Demo" section
   - Add your name and school info

3. **Share**
   - GitHub link: `https://github.com/yourusername/financial-analytics-dashboard`
   - Live app: `https://yourusername-financial-analytics.streamlit.app`
